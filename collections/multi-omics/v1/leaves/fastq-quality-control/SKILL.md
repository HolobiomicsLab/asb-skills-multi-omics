# fastq-quality-control

## Summary

Runs FastQC on simulated paired-end FASTQ files (e.g. the disease and normal samples produced by SimuSCoP in eduomics) and aggregates the reports with MultiQC before downstream variant calling, ensuring the simulated reads are fit for teaching variant calling workflows.

## When to use

Use when paired-end FASTQ files have just been simulated or received (e.g. SimuSCoP output from the eduomics DNA branch) and quality must be assessed before variant calling or other downstream analysis.

## When NOT to use

- The input is already a variant-called VCF or BAM — the question is variant detection (simulated_validated.vcf), not read quality.
- The data are RNA-seq count matrices from the eduomics RNA branch (Polyester/Salmon/DESeq2), where validation is based on differential expression significance rather than read QC of variant injection.
- No FASTQ files exist yet and the goal is simulation setup (reference subsetting, ClinVar variant selection, sequencing profile generation) rather than quality assessment.

## Inputs

- Simulated paired-end FASTQ files for the disease sample (SimuSCoP output with injected pathogenic variants)
- Simulated paired-end FASTQ files for the normal sample
- Nextflow samplesheet parameters (e.g. id, type=dna, chromosome, coverage) specifying the simulation

## Outputs

- Per-sample FastQC quality reports for the simulated FASTQ files
- Aggregated MultiQC quality report
- QC-passed FASTQ files carried forward to alignment and variant calling in the DNAVALIDATION module

## How to apply

Run FastQC on both the simulated disease and normal paired-end FASTQ files generated by SimuSCoP 2.0.1, then aggregate the per-sample reports with MultiQC to obtain a single consolidated quality report. In the eduomics DNAVALIDATION module this QC step is executed before aligning reads and calling variants against the subset GATK.GRCh38-derived reference bundle, because simulated data must meet quality standards before the pipeline will accept them. The QC step is a gate within a Nextflow (>=25.04.4) workflow executed under a containerized profile (Docker, Singularity, or Conda); poor-quality simulated FASTQ would undermine the subsequent check that the injected pathogenic variant is detectable in the disease sample and absent from the normal sample.

## Related tools

- **FastQC** (Per-base and per-read quality analysis of the simulated paired-end FASTQ files before variant calling)
- **MultiQC** (Aggregates the FastQC reports of the disease and normal simulated samples into a single quality report)
- **Nextflow** (Orchestrates the QC step within the DNAVALIDATION module of nf-core/eduomics (version >=25.04.4)) — https://github.com/lescai-teaching/eduomics

## Examples

```
nextflow run eduomics -profile docker --input samplesheet.csv --genome GATK.GRCh38 --outdir OUTDIR
```

## Evaluation signals

- FastQC reports exist for both the simulated disease and normal paired-end FASTQ files, not just one of the two samples.
- A consolidated MultiQC report is produced aggregating the per-sample FastQC results before any alignment or calling.
- QC occurs upstream of variant calling: reads only proceed to the validation step that emits simulated_validated.vcf after the quality reports are generated.
- The FASTQ files under QC are SimuSCoP-derived paired-end files in which pathogenic variants were injected into the disease sample.
- The workflow runs under a containerized Nextflow profile (Docker, Singularity, or Conda) with Nextflow >=25.04.4, ensuring reproducible environments.

## Limitations

- Read QC alone does not verify the ground truth; the injected pathogenic variant must additionally be confirmed by variant calling (present in disease, absent in normal) before the simulation is accepted.
- Only simulations passing validation are included in the final output, so a QC pass does not guarantee inclusion if the variant-detection check fails.
- The eduomics simulations are chromosome-specific (typically chr22) and capture-based, so QC profiles reflect targeted sequencing data rather than whole-genome data.
